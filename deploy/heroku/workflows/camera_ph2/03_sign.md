## Step 3 — Sign the PDF

Upload the PDF from Step 2 and apply your signature.

**What to do:**

1. Upload the PDF you downloaded from Step 2.
2. Follow the PDF Sign tool's on-screen prompts to place your signature
   and any required fields.
3. Download the signed PDF when prompted.

That's it — the signed file is the final artifact for this workflow.

> 💡 **Tip:** If you need to redo a step, use **⬅️ Previous step** at the
> bottom. Each tool keeps its own state, so re-running step 2 won't lose
> what you did in step 1.
