## Step 1 — Clean photos

Upload the original site photos you want to redact. The tool detects people
and vehicles and blurs/inpaints them automatically.

**What to do:**

1. Drag in your `.jpg` / `.png` photos.
2. Open the **⚙️ Settings** expander and pick the effect you want — `auto` is
   a good default (people inpainted, vehicles pixelated).
3. Click **🔍 Detect objects** on each tab, then **✨ Process image**.
4. When all photos are processed, click **⬇️ Download all cleaned images
   (.zip)** at the bottom.

**Hand-off to the next step:** keep that downloaded ZIP — Step 2 takes it as
input to build the PDF.
