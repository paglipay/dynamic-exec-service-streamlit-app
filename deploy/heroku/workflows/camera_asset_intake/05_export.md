## Step 5: Export

Download the confirmed data, shaped like the Asset Workbook's
`New_Equipment` sheet (CSV or XLSX).

This tool does not write back into your original `.xlsm` file — LAUSD's
turnover template has its own dropdowns, macros, and sign-off sheets
(`Assets_T_and_A`) that this export doesn't try to reproduce. Use this
as a reference to paste the MAC/serial/IP/camera-number values into the
real workbook by hand, or as an audit trail of what was scanned and
assigned.
